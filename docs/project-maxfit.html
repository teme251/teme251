AI APPLICATION / CONVERSATIONAL UI

MaxFit AI chatbot

A fitness-domain conversational AI prototype exploring question interpretation, focused responses, and a usable follow-up experience.

Conversational AIQuestion-answeringInteraction designResponse evaluation

Problem & intended value

A conversational product must make a broad topic easier to navigate. MaxFit explores a focused question-and-answer experience for users seeking fitness information.

Implementation

The prototype provides a conversational interface for fitness-related questions. Its application focus is the interaction between the user’s question, the returned response, and the flow of the ongoing conversation.

Technical approach

Fitness question → conversational response → follow-up. The technical focus is the application experience: question interpretation, response presentation, and conversation flow.

Engineering considerations

Questions vary in specificity and personal context. The key design challenge is responding usefully without treating incomplete information as a basis for overconfident, universal guidance.

Evaluation approach

Review representative questions for relevance, consistency, and clarity. Identify situations that require clarification or qualified human guidance. The focus is a coherent, appropriately scoped conversational experience.
