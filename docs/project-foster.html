AI APPLICATION / SUPPORT TOOL

Foster Caregiver Conversational AI Support

A domain-focused conversational AI prototype for Angels Among Us Pet Rescue, designed to connect caregiver questions with context-relevant guidance. The work centers on response relevance, dialogue design, and clear boundaries for an applied support experience.

Applied conversational AIDomain-focused Q&ADialogue designWeb applicationResponse evaluation

Application objective

Provide a conversational point of access for foster-caregiver questions. The prototype brings common support needs into a focused interaction in which the user can describe a situation and continue with follow-up questions.

Engineering contribution

The web application organizes the support experience around caregiver FAQs and tailored responses. The project connects domain context with conversational interaction, with attention to how the user expresses a question and understands the answer.

Interaction architecture

The application follows a question–response–follow-up structure. Its design centers on the relationship between user intent, contextual relevance, and the presentation of the response within a usable web experience.

Technical significance

A domain-specific assistant must do more than produce fluent text. The engineering considerations include relevance, unsupported assertions, uncertainty, and the point at which a conversation should move to qualified human support.

Evaluation approach

Use representative caregiver questions to review contextual relevance, clarity, unsupported assertions, and escalation needs. Evaluate the interaction against the needs of the caregiver and the limits of the available information.
