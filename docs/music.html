03 / BEHIND THE WORK

Culture in the words.
Curiosity in the process.

“I directed the stories and visual world, using AI as an instrument to bring the music to life.”

QENÉ & CODE brings together Ethiopian language, personal experiences, beliefs, and emotion. Under the artist name Teme251, I shape the lyrics, creative direction, and visual identity around a point of view that is my own.

The work extends from the songs into cover concepts, lyric visuals, and release creative. Technology helps build that world; the stories give it meaning.

Writing & direction ＋

Personal stories and Ethiopian cultural perspective guide the words, themes, and creative choices across the album.

AI-enabled production ＋

I use AI as a production instrument, guiding and selecting material around the intent of each song. The album is an exploration of how human direction can shape a new creative workflow.

Visual storytelling ＋

The release includes visual identity, lyric visuals, and promotional creative. Follow the YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook pages for the work surrounding the music.
